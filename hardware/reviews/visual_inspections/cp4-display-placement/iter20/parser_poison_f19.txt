CP4 iteration 20 — F19 poison suite
====================================================================

Two fail-open edges in the direct-child parser:
  (1) the boolean side reader returned False — i.e. FRONT — for absent,
      malformed AND non-copper layers alike, so unknown was indistinguishable
      from a valid front-side footprint;
  (2) the child walk used a paren counter that treated parentheses inside
      QUOTED STRINGS as structure, so a legal quoted description with an
      unmatched paren hid every later top-level field.
The reviewer established (2) is not a synthetic grammar assumption: KiCad 10
parses such a board without complaint. These boards already carry 4361
(battery) and 1727 (display) quoted parentheses — benign only because they
happen to be balanced pairs.
