Reference-text model
--------------------
This model is independent of the designer's 0.95-character/1.45-height
label-adjacency gate. It parses the written board and models each visible
reference with:

  width  = max(character_count * 0.88 * nominal_font_height, 1.0 mm)
  height = nominal_font_height

Footprint rotation is applied to the property anchor. Text rotation swaps
the box axes. A pair is flagged when it overlaps on one axis and has less
than 0.20 mm edge clearance on the other.

Visible references checked: 123
Flagged pairs: 0

The independent KiCad renders and eight crops agree: L10/R23 and L12/R33
are now visually distinct.

Isolation pad-edge model
------------------------
The written board was parsed independently with KiCad's +Y-down rotation.
Unconnected pads were skipped. U10/U11/C28/C38 were excluded as the
documented barrier crossings. Connected nets beginning BUS_,
ISO_BUS_GND, V_ISO, GND2_DCDC, TV, or PACK were assigned by channel;
all other connected pads were logic. The metric is Euclidean center
distance minus half the maximum X/Y pad extent at both ends.

  logic to iso1: 1.9400 mm
    J6.SH [GND] to J10.SH [ISO_BUS_GND1]
  logic to iso2: 9.6787 mm
    C31.1 [VCC2] to R37.2 [PACK2_Bminus]
  iso1 to iso2: 2.6999 mm
    L11.1 [GND2_DCDC1] to R32.1 [BUS_A2]

These reproduce packet section 3 and D38 after rounding.
