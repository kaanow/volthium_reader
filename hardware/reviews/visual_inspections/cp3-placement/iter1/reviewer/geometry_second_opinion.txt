Method
------
This check does not use the designer's refine_refdes.py collision model.
It parses the written board, converts each visible reference's local
coordinates to board coordinates, and models a reference as a fixed box:

  width  = character_count * text_height * 0.88
  height = text_height

Pairs are flagged when their boxes overlap on one axis and have less than
0.20 mm edge clearance on the other. The direct KiCad render and crop are
then the deciding visual layer.
